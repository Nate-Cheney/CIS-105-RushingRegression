What is RYOE?

Rushing Yards Over Expected (RYOE) measures how many yards a running back gains compared to what would be expected based on the distance to go for a first down. It uses a regression model where:

Expected Yards = (Yards to Go × 0.126) + 3.473

A positive RYOE indicates the player is performing better than expected, while a negative RYOE suggests underperformance.
